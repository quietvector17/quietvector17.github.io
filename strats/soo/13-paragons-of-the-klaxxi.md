## Paragons of the Klaxxi (WIP)

### Overview

- Council-style fight with `9` bosses total.
- Only `3` Paragons are active at a time.
  - When one dies, the next one joins the fight.
- Main goals:
  - Kill priority targets in a planned order.
  - Handle each Paragon's signature mechanic cleanly.
  - Keep the room controlled as abilities begin to overlap.

#### Heroic notes

- Heroic is mostly the same fight, but every overlap is much less forgiving.
- Kill order and cooldown planning matter a lot more than on normal.
- A bad `Aim`, missed `Bloodletting`, failed `Mesmerize`, or sloppy `Catalyst` often snowballs into a wipe.
- Use a fixed plan pull to pull instead of improvising.

### General Notes

- Keep the raid loosely spread unless a mechanic calls for stacking.
- Swap attention quickly when a high-priority add or amber target appears.
- Tanks should be ready for repeated active-mitigation checks.
  - Several Paragons punish tanks very hard if mitigation is not up.
- Do not split damage across multiple kill targets without a reason.
  - This fight gets much easier when dangerous mechanics leave the fight quickly.

#### Heroic positioning notes

- Heroic: spreading is more important because overlaps are what kill you.
- Heroic: assign exact spots for ranged when possible.
  - This makes `Aim`, `Catalyst`, and `Insane Calculation` much cleaner.
- Heroic: pre-assign external cooldowns for tanks during dangerous overlap windows.

### Common Kill Order

- Kill order varies by group, but a common plan is:
  - `Skeer` -> `Rik'kal` -> `Hisek` -> `Korven` -> `Xaril` -> `Kaz'tik` -> `Kil'ruk` -> `Iyyokuk` -> `Ka'roz`
- The exact middle/end can vary.
  - Keep your order consistent once you choose one.

### Priority Mechanics

- `Bloodletting` (`Skeer the Bloodseeker`)
  - Bloods fixate and try to reach their target.
  - If they connect, they heal the Paragons and create extra pressure.
  - Slow, stun, and kill them immediately.
  - Heroic: blood control has to be instant; late swaps or weak slows quickly snowball.

- `Injection` (`Rik'kal the Dissector`)
  - Dangerous debuff that needs fast handling.
  - Follow your group's dispel / immunity plan.
  - Do not let parasite-related mechanics get loose in the raid.
  - Heroic: this gets much more dangerous when it overlaps with other movement mechanics.

- `Aim` (`Hisek the Swarmkeeper`)
  - A player gets targeted by a long yellow beam.
  - Assigned players stand in the line to split the hit.
  - Use personals and externals when needed.
  - Do not take the shot alone.
  - Heroic: use a fixed soak order so the same players are not improvising every cast.

- `Shield Bash` / amber heal (`Korven the Prime`)
  - Korven can encase himself or another Paragon in amber.
  - Swap and break the amber quickly before the target recovers too much health.
  - Heroic: save burst and cleave for amber breaks; losing time here usually causes later overlap wipes.

- `Catalyst` (`Xaril the Poisoned Mind`)
  - Players receive color-based debuffs.
  - When `Catalyst` happens, execute your assigned color movement correctly.
  - This is one of the easiest wipe points if people panic or move through each other.
  - Heroic: this is one of the biggest execution checks in the fight.

- `Mesmerize` (`Kaz'tik the Manipulator`)
  - A player gets controlled and pulled toward a hungry kunchong.
  - Break the control immediately with your raid's assigned response.
  - Do not let the mesmerized player reach the bug.
  - Heroic: assign who breaks `Mesmerize` before the pull; delayed reactions are usually fatal.

- `Reave` (`Kil'ruk the Wind-Reaver`)
  - Creates a dangerous path through the raid.
  - Move out of the path and keep it from cutting off too much room.
  - Heroic: bad `Reave` placement can force deaths on the next movement mechanic.

- `Insane Calculation` (`Iyyokuk the Lucid`)
  - Links players based on matching traits.
  - Spread and identify who you are linked to.
  - Do not stand on top of matching players.
  - Heroic: this is much deadlier when combined with `Catalyst` or `Aim` movement.

- `Hurl Amber` / mobility pressure (`Ka'roz the Locust`)
  - Causes extra movement and makes the room messier late in the fight.
  - Stay disciplined with positioning once he is active.
  - Heroic: late-fight movement errors are common because the raid is already strained by previous overlaps.

### Paragon Notes

#### `Skeer the Bloodseeker`

- Usually killed early.
- `Bloodletting` is the main problem.
- Bloods are top priority over boss damage.
- Heroic: if bloods are slow to die, healing and kill timing both get much worse immediately.

#### `Rik'kal the Dissector`

- Often the second kill.
- Watch `Injection` handling carefully.
- Tanks should respect his active-mitigation check.
- Heroic: parasite mistakes are much harder to recover from.

#### `Hisek the Swarmkeeper`

- `Aim` is the major mechanic.
- Pre-assign an `Aim` group so nobody hesitates.
- If your group uses immunities or strong personals here, plan that in advance.
- Heroic: `Aim` overlaps are one of the main reasons to keep positioning clean.

#### `Korven the Prime`

- Save burst to break amber quickly.
- If amber is not broken fast, you lose too much time and damage.
- Heroic: many groups treat Korven as a high-priority kill largely because amber recovery is so punishing.

#### `Xaril the Poisoned Mind`

- Color mechanics must be called clearly.
- If your raid has a standard color map, follow it exactly every pull.
- Heroic: sloppy color movement will wipe the raid even when healing looks stable beforehand.

#### `Kaz'tik the Manipulator`

- `Mesmerize` needs instant reaction.
- Hungry kunchongs are a control problem more than a DPS race.
- Heroic: this is one of the mechanics that should feel scripted, not reactive.

#### `Kil'ruk the Wind-Reaver`

- Keep `Reave` from cutting the raid in half.
- Movement mistakes are usually what make this Paragon dangerous.
- Heroic: `Reave` placement often decides whether the next mechanic is manageable.

#### `Iyyokuk the Lucid`

- Spread for `Insane Calculation`.
- Call matching links cleanly and move early.
- Heroic: panic movement here creates chain deaths fast.

#### `Ka'roz the Locust`

- Usually easier once only a few Paragons remain, but he still punishes bad movement.
- Stay tidy and finish the fight.
- Heroic: do not relax just because the boss count is lower; this is where cleanup mistakes still kill pulls.

### Heroic Execution Notes

- Heroic rewards killing the most disruptive mechanics as early as possible.
- Have fixed assignments for:
  - `Aim` soakers
  - `Mesmerize` breaks
  - `Catalyst` movement
  - blood/add control
- Do not burn raid cooldowns randomly.
  - Save them for known overlap points.
- Common dangerous overlap examples:
  - `Aim` during heavy add pressure
  - `Catalyst` with poor spacing
  - `Mesmerize` while players are already moving for another mechanic
- Heroic cooldown pacing:
  - Use raid CDs on known overlap sets, not on random damage.
  - Save burst for amber breaks and dangerous kill-order checkpoints.
  - Save personals for your assigned mechanic first, not just whenever you drop low.
- Heroic kill-order note:
  - The point of the kill order is to remove the mechanics your raid handles the worst.
  - If you are wiping to one specific overlap repeatedly, change the order rather than forcing the same pattern forever.
- If a pull is failing repeatedly, the first thing to clean up is usually execution order, not raw DPS.
