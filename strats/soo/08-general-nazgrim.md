## General Nazgrim

- waves of 2 adds will appear every 45sec

### Adds

- Korkron Ironblades
  - Warrior add
  - Casts `Ironstorm` continuously (Bladestorm)
    - Do NOT stand in these (unless you're the tank)

- Korkron Assassins
  - Rogue Add
  - Start the fight in stealth and fixate a player until the player dies
  - Will use `backstab` if they can get behind you
  - Can use Hunter Flare to reveal them early
  - Stunnable, Slowable, Snareable

- Korkron Arcweaver
  - Mage Add
  - Randomly `blink` around the room
  - `??? Strike`
    - Targeted AoE Ability
  - `Arcing Shock`
    - Damages a random player
    - 25% damage increase for each subsequent cast

- Korkron Warshaman
  - Healers
  - `Chain heal` (MUST be interrupted)
  - `Earth shield` (must be dispelled)
  - `Healing totems`
    - move mobs away from totems AND Kill totems

- HEROIC: Korkron Sniper
  - Fixates (Hunter's Mark) on a random player and fire a volley towards them
  - 400K Physical damage in a 40 yard cone
  - When Fixated - YOU MUST FACE THE ADD TOWARDS A WALL

### Boss

- Has a rage bar that gets filled up by certain abilities
- Will cast the most expensive ability available to him every 15s

#### Rage Abilities

- 30 Rage
  - `Heroic Shockwave`
    - Leaps on to a random player, dealing physical damage to them and anyone nearby
    - Three fiery lines will move out from the impact point
    - Spread out

- 50 Rage
  - `Korkron Banner`
    - While the banner is up, all attacks from adds will grant the boss rage
    - !ABSOLUTE NUMBER ONE PRIORITY WHILE THEY'RE UP!

- 70 Rage
  - `War Song`
    - Physical damage to whole raid equal to half your health
    - use raid CD here

- 100 Rage
  - `Ravager`
    - Axe that spins around the room and deals high damage
    - If you get hit, the boss gains rage
    - !AVOID LETTING THE BOSS GETTING 100 RAGE AT ALL COSTS!

#### Other Abilities

- Also casts `Bonecracker`
  - Random cast a player
  - Reduces max health by 10%

- Also casts `Sundering Blow` (Tank ability)
  - Grants 5 rage
  - Applies armor reduction buff on tank that stacks
    - Each stack causes an extra 5 rage
    - 3 stacks = 20 rage
    - Tanks with high avoidance help with this mechanic (BM Monk, Guardian Druid)
    - Advisable to 3 tank this to keep stacks low

### Stances

- Last for 1 minute each
- Will always rotate stances in the same order
  - Battle -> Berserker -> Defensive

- `Battle Stance`
  - Gains 1 rage per second

- `Berserker Stance`
  - Deal AND take 25% more damage
  - !!PUMP BOSS HERE!!
  - HEROIC: Still kill Arc Weavers, Shamans, and Assassins
  - HEROIC: can safely ignore Snipers and Iron blades until `Berserker Stance` is over.
  - HEROIC: Use lust on 2nd `Berserker Stance`

- `Defensive Stance`
  - Take 25% less damage and all attacks generate Rage
  - DO NOT ATTACK BOSS
  - Pets can attack safely.

### Priority

- War Banners > Healing Tide Totems > War Shamans > Arc Weavers > Assassins

- HEROIC: War Banners > Healing Tide Totems > Arc Weavers > War Shamans > Assassins > Snipers > Iron Blades
  - Rotate stuns on Arc Weavers while killing them
  - War Shamans need to be constantly interrupted and moved
  - !!DO NOT STUN MOBS ON TOP OF HEALING TIDE TOTEMS!!
  - Assassins should be revealed and kited with a slow while other units are killed
  - Tanks should pick up Iron blades and then ignore them
  - Only DPS Iron blades during `Defensive Stance`\

- HEROIC: Ignore all adds and focus boss at 10%
