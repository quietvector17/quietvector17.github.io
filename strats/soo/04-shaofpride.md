## Sha of Pride

### Pride Basics

- Pride is the fight's main mechanic.
  - Many abilities increase your Pride.
  - The UI power bar represents your current Pride.

### Key Debuffs

- `Gift of the Titans`
  - Applied to 8 players.
  - While you have it, you cannot gain Pride.
  - Stack the buffed players together to trigger the group effect.
    - Increases damage/healing/haste for 15s.
- `Mark of Arrogance`
  - Applied to 5 random players.
  - Deals damage until dispelled.
  - Dispel: the dispeller gains 5 Pride.

### Boss Energy (1-100)

- Boss energy fills over ~1 minute and triggers at specific thresholds.
- 35 Energy: `Self Reflection`
  - Creates a dark pool underneath each player.
  - Pool explodes after ~2 seconds, spawning a small add.
    - Tanks pick these up.
- 65 Energy: `Imprison`
  - Traps 4 players in the 4 corners around the boss.
  - Freeing them:
    - 3 players must stand on the yellow glowing floor panels.
  - Tanks and healers cannot be imprisoned.
  - After `Imprison`, boss spawns 3 `Manifestation of Pride` adds.
    - Must be interrupted or silenced.
    - Stunnable and Death Grippable.
- 100 Energy: `Swelling Pride`
  - Grants 5 Pride to everyone hit.
  - Additional effects based on your Pride:
    - **25 Pride**: swirly effect underneath you, move out.
    - **50 Pride**: projection spawns within 15 yards and explodes.
      - ***Move to your void zone (green arrow marker).***
    - **75 Pride**: `Aura of Pride` for 25s.
      - Pulsing AoE around you; get away from people.
    - **100 Pride**: permanently mind controlled.
      - First time you reach 100: +50% damage and healing for 15s.

#### Heroic notes

- `Rifts of Corruption` spawn around the room.
  - Run over them to close them.
  - Each player can only close 1 rift per minute.
  - Everyone should be actively closing rifts.

#### Heroic: Banishment realm

- You leave a copy of yourself in the outside realm.
  - Raid can damage it to break you out.
  - Jumping into golden orbs damages your outside-realm copy.
- You are compelled to always move forward.
- Hitting walls or any Sha results in death.
