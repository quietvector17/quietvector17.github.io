### Overview

- Mostly a trash/waves fight.
- Goal:
  - Clear waves + minibosses.
  - Capture both towers.
  - Use the tower turrets to bring Galakras down.
  - Kill Galakras on the ground.

### Add Priority (General)

- `Tidal Shaman`
  - Stun and interrupt as much as possible.
  - Kill `Healing Totem` immediately (Melee Priority).
- `Flag Bearer`
  - Kill `War Banner` immediately (Melee Priority).
- `Bonecrusher`
  - Must be stunned/controlled and interrupted.
  - Charges a friendly NPC: taunt or interrupt to stop it.

### Wave / Ground Notes

- Move out of gray clouds on the floor (from `Ebon Stalker`).
- `Korgra the Snake`
  - Unlocks the tower door.
  - Spawns a `Demolisher`.
  - Kill `Korgra` and the `Demolisher` before committing to the tower.

### Tower 1 (South)

#### Miniboss priority (outside)

- `Ebon Stalker`
- `Korgra the Snake`
- `Demolisher`
- Then go up the South tower.

#### Inside the tower

- Kill the miniboss and all adds.
- Once tower trash is cleared:
  - One player can stay up and use the turret to kill all `Proto-drake`s.
  - After drakes are dead, click the rope to drop it for the raid.

#### Heroic notes

- The tower will unlock automatically after a certain amount of time.
- Protect the demolition crew that is trying to blow open the door.
- Suggested South tower team: 3 DPS, 2 healers, 1 tank.
  - Priority:
    - Grunts hitting the crew at the door.
    - Then help main team with adds.

### Tower 2 (North)

#### Miniboss priority (outside)

- `Tidal Shaman`
- Dodge cleaves/frontals.
- `Demolisher`
- Then go up the North tower.

#### Inside the tower

- Same process as South.

#### Heroic notes

- The tower will unlock automatically after a certain amount of time.
- Protect the demolition crew that is trying to blow open the door.
- North tower team should be ready to climb when it unlocks.

### Turrets / Bringing the Boss Down

- Send one person to the South tower turret when the North tower is almost clear.
- Both turret users fire at the same time to bring Galakras down.
  - This also halts add spawns.

### Boss Ground Phase

- `Skull Cracker`
  - Pulls players in.
  - Face the boss away from the raid.
  - If you get pulled in: run away immediately or you die.

- Fire pulse mechanic (similar to Iron Qon Ro'shak fire smash)
  - Pulses for ~7 seconds.
  - Each pulse does more damage.
  - Each pulse grants the boss a permanent 2% fire damage increase.

- Fire orb mechanic
  - Boss targets a player with a beam, then shoots a fire orb at that player.
  - Each player the orb passes through:
    - Reduces the orb's damage.
    - Takes a ticking fire DoT.
  - Stand in the beam to soak if you do not already have heavy stacks of the DoT.

#### Heroic notes

- Split into two groups on either hind leg.
- `Flames of Galakrond` target: run behind your group to reduce orb damage.
- Let stacks reset by alternating which group people run behind.
