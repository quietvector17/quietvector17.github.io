## Malkorok

### Overview

- Two repeating phases:
  - Phase 1: `Ancient Miasma` (all healing becomes an absorb shield)
  - Phase 2: `Blood Rage` (you lose shields and heal normally)

### Phase 1: Ancient Miasma ("Spread Phase")

- Healing is converted into a shield on the target (max = target max health).
  - Shield color: Red -> Yellow -> Green (strength)

- `Arcing Smash`
  - Cone slam that hits a section of the room a few seconds after the cast.
  - Put raid markers down for the 3 smash locations so people pre-position.
  - After three `Arcing Smash` casts, boss uses `Breath of Y'Shaarj`.
  - After the 3rd `Arcing Smash` but before `Breath of Y'Shaarj`, boss casts `Imploding Energy`.
    - Soak `Imploding Energy` first, then get away from the marked smash zones.

- `Breath of Y'Shaarj`
  - The last 3 `Arcing Smash` locations explode for massive damage.
  - Move out of the 3 marked zones.
  - Heroic: after the 2nd `Breath of Y'Shaarj`, move into melee range.

- `Imploding Energy` (void zones)
  - On 25m: 7 void zones spawn.
  - One player must soak each within ~4 seconds.
    - Soaking deals ~450K shadow damage.
    - If a zone is not soaked: ~500K damage to the entire raid.
  - Try to have a full shield before soaking; use a defensive if your shield is low.
  - If you see an unsoaked zone: it is better to die soaking than let it hit the raid.

- `Seismic Slam`
  - Targets a random player.
  - Deals ~500K physical damage to the target and anyone within 5 yards.
  - Spread 5+ yards.

#### Heroic: adds / orbs

- `Living Corruption` (from `Seismic Slam`)
  - Spawns under the targeted player.
  - 8 yard aura massively slows movement and casting speed.
  - Grip/taunt into melee so it can be cleaved down.

- `Essence of Y'Shaarj`
  - Running into these removes your shield and deals ~150K damage.
  - Avoid them; healing does not restore real HP during `Ancient Miasma`.
  - Some classes with strong personals/immunities can rocket boots and soak multiples (rogue/warlock/SP/hunter).

### Phase 2: Blood Rage ("Split Phase")

- Starts when boss reaches 100 rage (~2 minutes).
- `Ancient Miasma` ends: shields are removed and you can heal normally.

- Positioning:
  - Stack in front of the boss with tanks.
  - Boss cleaves for very high damage; the goal is to split it across the stack.

- `Displaced Energy`
  - Deals ~337.5K shadow damage every 3 seconds for 9 seconds.
  - When it ends or is dispelled: deals ~607.5K shadow damage to all players within 8 yards.
  - If you get it: get out, then call for a dispel.
  - Heroic: also snares.
    - Use snare breaks (`Hand of Freedom`, `Blink`, `Unbound Will`, etc.).
    - Often needs 2x `Stampeding Roar` to cover both casts in this phase.
