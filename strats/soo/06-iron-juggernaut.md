## Iron Juggernaut

### Overview

- Two main phases:
  - `Assault Mode` (boss melees)
  - `Siege Mode` (boss rooted)
- Heroic: everything hits harder.

### Assault Mode

- Boss melees the tank.
- `Flame Vents`
  - Frontal cone.
  - Applies a stacking debuff (fire damage + increased fire damage taken).
- `Laser Burn`
  - Random players.
  - Applies a 16 second Fire DoT (~25K DPS)
- `Demolisher Cannons`
  - Constant damage to random players.
  - ~165K fire damage to the target and players within 6 yards.
  - Spread.
- `Mortar Cannons`
  - Large pink circle under a player.
  - ~300K damage to anyone in it.
  - Hard to dodge if you are the target: react immediately.
- `Borer Drill`
  - Follows players for a few seconds.
  - Continuous physical damage.
  - Keep moving or tank it.
- `Crawler Mines`
  - 5 mines begin a 10s `Detonation Sequence`.
  - If a cast completes: ~300K damage to the entire raid.
  - If you interrupt/soak the detonation: ~1.5M physical damage to the soaker.
    - Use defensives.
    - Clothies ~1M, tanks ~600K.

#### Heroic notes

- `Ricochet`
  - Saw blade moving through raiders.
  - ~350K damage + knockback.
  - Spread out and try to avoid (hard).

### Siege Mode (after full energy, ~2 minutes)

- Boss stops meleeing and is rooted.
- Continues casting:
  - `Crawler Mines`
  - `Demolisher Cannons`
- Raid-wide nature damage pulses.
- `Shock Pulse`
  - Damage + huge knockback.
  - **Put your back to walls/terrain**.
- Tar
  - Thrown at random nearby locations.
  - Low to moderate nature damage.
  - 30% slow while standing in it.
- Fixating laser
  - Targets random players.
  - Kite it around the room.
  - **Keep the laser out of tar puddles at all costs**.

#### Heroic notes

- `Mortar Barrage`
  - Many `Mortar Cannons`.
  - Prioritize dodging.
