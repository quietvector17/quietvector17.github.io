## Thok the Bloodthirsty

### Overview

- Heroic: 2 tanks + 7 healers.
- Heroic: boss has much more health.
- Fight alternates between:
  - Stack phase
  - Frenzy / kite phase
- Heroic phase order:
  - Normal stack -> Normal kite -> Poison stack -> Poison kite -> Frost stack -> Frost kite -> Fire stack -> Fire kite

#### Heroic notes

- Split the raid into 2 groups, with no more than 14 players in each group.
  - This prevents Thok from phasing early due to `Bloodied` proximity.
- Possible pacing:
  - Can push very high on the first stack phase (`>29`).
  - Only do ~11-12 on the second.
  - Cooldowns should be back for the third / last stack phase.
- Ranged should follow Thok while he is fixating players.
- Melee should focus the `Kor'kron Jailer`.
- Open the cage 2-3 stacks before your desired transition.
- Cave bats fly down after the second screech.
  - Offtank picks them up and brings them into melee for cleave.
  - Use second potions + raid CDs to kill the bats.
  - They should be dead by the 10th `Deafening Screech`.
- Stack on a marker to avoid the yeti.
  - You can either kill it or ignore it.
- If boss is at ~12% by Frost kite phase, you can ignore the jailer and burn the boss.

### Stack Phase

- `Fearsome Roar`
  - Frontal breath; should only hit tanks.
  - Causes the tank to take 25% more damage per stack.
- `Tail Lash`
  - Hits anyone standing behind the boss.
  - Stand on his sides to avoid both of these abilities.
- `Shock Blast`
  - ~100K nature damage to several random players.
- `Deafening Screech`
  - Cast whenever Thok reaches full energy.
  - Deals physical damage to the whole raid.
  - Interrupts spellcasting for 2 seconds.
  - Thok gains 1 stack of `Acceleration` each time he casts it.
    - `Acceleration` increases Thok's energy regeneration rate.

### `Bloodied`

- Whenever a player drops below 50% health, they get the `Bloodied` debuff.
- If 15 players with `Bloodied` are within 10 yards of each other, Thok transitions to frenzy and resets his stacks.
- Keep him in stack phase as long as you can keep everyone alive.
- Baseline note: around 12 stacks, stop raid healing around 9 and only heal players who are too low to survive another hit.
  - Heroic: aim more like 26-29 stacks on the first push.

### Frenzy Phase

- Thok fixates random raid members and chases them for 12 seconds.
  - Anyone near Thok's head will be killed.
  - After that, he repeats and fixates someone else.
- If you are fixated: run toward the entrance.
  - Everyone else runs the opposite direction.
  - Kite away from the raid.
- Tanks pick up the spawned `Kor'kron Jailer`.
  - The jailer drops a key.
  - Use the key to open a cage (`1` left, `2` right).
  - Thok eats that NPC, which ends frenzy and starts the next stack phase.

### Akolik (Poison)

- Left cage: free first.
- `Fearsome Roar` -> `Acid Breath`
  - Nature damage instead of physical.
  - Reduces armor with each stack.
- `Shock Blast` -> `Corrosive Blood`
  - Nature damage to the raid.
  - Applies a 30 second nature DoT.

### Waterspeaker Gorai (Frost)

- `Fearsome Roar` -> `Freezing Breath`
  - Frost damage.
  - Freezes the tank in ice at 5 stacks.
- `Shock Blast` -> `Icy Blood`
  - Stacking frost debuff.
  - Players are frozen at 5 stacks.
  - This is completely random.

### Warmaster Montak (Fire)

- Back-right cage: free last.
- Ideally boss should be below `20%` when starting fire phase.
- `Fearsome Roar` -> `Scorching Breath`
  - Deals fire damage.
  - Also deals additional fire damage every 2 seconds.
- `Shock Blast` -> `Burning Blood`
  - Deals damage to random raid members.
  - Leaves a fire puddle underneath them.
  - In the last stack phase, the room will get covered with fire pools.
    - Spread them around the room, or
    - Chain the rest of your cooldowns and kill the boss.
