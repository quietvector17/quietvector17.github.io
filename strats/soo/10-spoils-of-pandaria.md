## Spoils of Pandaria

### Overview

- Add fight.
- Each room contains crates that spawn mobs.
  - Each mob you kill releases `Titan Energy`, which fills a meter.
  - Once both initial rooms reach 50 energy, you can activate the lever to open the next room.
  - Rooms explode after 4.5 minutes, wiping the raid.

#### Heroic notes

- Every time you kill an add in one room, an `Unstable Spark` spawns in the other room.
  - It begins a 10 second cast when it spawns.
  - If not killed, it explodes for ~500K damage.

### Pandaren Crates

- 2 in each room.
- Green panda paw print.
- Provide no `Titan Energy`, but are crucial because of the buffs they give.
- Can spawn 1 of 3 Pandaren mobs; each drops an orb when it dies.
- Clicking the orb grants everyone of one role a buff.

- `Windwalker` (This is a Panda add that spawns, we do not need one in raid)
  - Charges and leaves a line of blossoms on the ground for 30s.
  - Buff: DPS gain a chance to deal bonus fire damage.

- `Brewmaster`
  - Spinning fire breath: dodge or outrange it.
  - Throws kegs that damage and slow.
  - Buff: tanks gain a chance to deal massive bonus damage in a small AoE and stun enemies.

- `Mistweaver`
  - `Gusting Crane Kick`
    - Pulls players toward her.
    - Damages players within 10 yards.
  - Buff: healers have a chance to send out a tidal wave that travels forward, healing players and damaging enemies.

### Heroic Room Plan

- For each room:
  - Start with 1 Pandaren chest and DPS it to ~40%, then open the other Pandaren chest.
    - Buffs are very important and are random each pull.
    - Most important buff is usually the healer buff, since it lets healers destroy `Unstable Spark`.
      - Healers should always face sparks.
  - After the first Pandaren mob dies and the second is at ~40%:
    - Open 1 large + 2 small.
    - Heroic: lust here for the Mogu room.
    - Keep opening small mobs 2-3 at a time while the large is still up.
  - After the large dies:
    - Keep opening the rest of the crates.
    - Example finishes:
      - 4-5 small
      - 2 medium + 2 small
      - Any similar combination

### Mantid Rooms

#### Normal chest order

- 1st medium
- 2 Pandaren
- 2nd medium
- Large mobs one at a time
  - While doing this, free small crates and cleave them down with the big adds.

#### Heroic chest order

- 1 large
- All mediums
- All smalls

#### Small crates

- 18 small crates in each room.
- Mobs produce 1 energy each.
- Can spawn 3 types of adds:
  - `Amber Encaser Kunchong`
    - Heroic: highest priority.
    - Stands still and shoots pheromones at random players, leaving a pool at their location.
    - Move out of the pool.
  - `Set'thik Bombardier`
    - Throws explosives at random targets for splash damage.
    - Spread out.
    - `Gusting Bomb`: move out of the green ground AoE.
  - `Kor'thik Warcaller`
    - Melees the tank and can enrage.
    - Kite while enraged.
    - Heroic: should be dispelled by a rogue or hunter.

#### Medium crates

- 6 medium crates in each room.
- Mobs produce 3 energy each.
- Can spawn 2 mob types:
  - `Set'thik Wind Wielder`
    - Summons tornadoes that move faster and faster around the room.
    - Stay out of tornado paths.
    - Dispel the buff they put on mobs.
    - Heroic: second-highest priority.
  - `Amber Priest`
    - Dispel or Spellsteal the healing buff they cast.

#### Large crates

- 2 large crates in each room.
- Mobs produce 14 energy each.
- `Mantid Commander`
  - Constant raid AoE damage.
  - Applies `Set to Blow`.
    - When you reach 4 stacks, use the Extra Action Button to place a bomb and reduce your stacks by 1.
    - Put these bombs out of the raid.

### Mogu Rooms

- Generally the easier side.

#### Normal chest order

- 4 small
- Pandaren crates
- All medium crates
  - Try to keep 2 up at once for cleave.
- Large crates one at a time

#### Heroic chest order

- 1 large
- All mediums
- All smalls

#### Small crates

- 18 small crates in each room.
- Mobs produce 1 energy each.
- Small-crate mobs to watch for:
  - Quillen pack
    - Applies a stacking DoT.
  - `Burial Urn`
    - Constantly spawns `Sparks of Life`.
    - Pulses continuously in a small AoE.
    - Slightly larger pulse on death.

#### Medium crates

- 6 medium crates in each room.
- Mobs produce 3 energy each.
- Can spawn 2 mob types:
  - `Anima Golem`
    - Creates red pools on the ground that heal mobs standing in them.
    - Creates pillars.
      - Pillars must be soaked by players.
  - `Shadow Ritualist`
    - Spawns with a phylactery; they share health.
    - `Forbidden Magic`
      - Interrupt this.
    - `Rune of Power`
      - Buffs haste by 100%.
      - Move mobs out of it.

#### Large crates

- 2 large crates in each room.
- Mobs produce 14 energy each.
- Large mogu differ only by element.
  - Continuously cast a large raid AoE spell.
  - Create stone statues around themselves that persist until killed.
    - Stone statues grant mobs a 10% damage bonus.
    - Kill the statues to keep them under control.
