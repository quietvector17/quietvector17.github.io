## Sha of Pride

### Overview

- 25-man setup: 2 tanks, 5-6 healers.
- Pride is the main resource.
  - Many mechanics add Pride.
  - Higher Pride adds extra effects to `Swelling Pride`.
  - At 100 Pride, a later `Swelling Pride` permanently mind controls the player.
- Boss cycles energy from 0 to 100 roughly every minute.
- At `30%`, all Pride and boss energy reset, then the final burn begins.

### Positioning

- Normal:
  - Tightly stack near the back of the room for healing, buffs, and raid CDs.
  - Tanks stand in front/slightly to the side.
  - Spread briefly for `Self Reflection`, then restack.
- Heroic:
  - Spread evenly around all four sides of the boss, but stay close enough for healing.
  - The spread is mainly to close `Rifts of Corruption` quickly.
  - If you have the rift-closing debuff, move closer to center for healing/dispels.

### Key Debuffs

- `Gift of the Titans`
  - Applied to 8 players in 25-man.
  - Players with it cannot gain Pride.
  - Gifted players stack together to trigger the damage/healing/haste buff.
  - Gifted players can also safely handle Pride-gaining jobs while the buff is active.
- `Mark of Arrogance`
  - Applied to 5 players in 25-man.
  - Deals heavy damage until dispelled.
  - Dispeller gains 5 Pride unless protected by `Gift of the Titans`.
  - Gifted healers dispel as top priority.
  - Any healer dispels if the player may die or `Swelling Pride` is soon.

### Energy Cycle

#### 35 Energy: Self Reflection

- Dark pool spawns under each player.
- Pool explodes after ~2 seconds and spawns a small add.
- Move a few yards out, then restack.
- Off tank gathers adds.
- AoE if clumped; multidot/kill stragglers.

#### 65 Energy: Imprison

- 4 players are trapped in the 4 prisons in 25-man.
- Trapped players take damage and gain Pride.
- Free each player by having 3 players stand on that prison's glowing panels until all turn blue.
- Assign prison teams before pull.
- Tanks and healers cannot be imprisoned, so use them heavily in assignments.
- Warlock gateways help reach far prisons.
- Move toward prison positions after 60 energy, but do not stand on prisons before the cast.
  - Standing too close when prison lands causes knockback, damage, and extra Pride.

#### After Imprison: Manifestation of Pride

- 3 large adds spawn in 25-man.
- Assign a silence/interrupt or Death Grip for each add.
- Move adds toward the raid/center and kill quickly.
- Interrupt `Mocking Blast`; it deals damage and gives Pride.
- Stuns and grips work.
- Avoid having a healer be the nearest player when a manifestation dies, because the nearest player gains Pride.

#### 100 Energy: Swelling Pride

- Heavy raid hit; use a raid cooldown when available.
- Gives 5 Pride to everyone hit.
- Extra effects based on Pride before the hit:
  - `25+`: swirl spawns under you; move out immediately after the hit.
  - `50+`: projection spawns within 15 yards; the affected player must stand in their green-arrow void zone before it explodes.
  - `75+`: `Aura of Pride`; stay away from everyone after the hit.
  - `100`: permanent mind control on a later `Swelling Pride`.

### Final Burn

- At `30%`, all Pride resets to 0 and boss energy resets.
- Boss starts pulsing raid damage every 10 seconds and adds Pride.
- Bloodlust at `30%`.
- Continue all normal mechanics; do not tunnel through prisons/adds/rifts.
- Chain raid cooldowns as damage ramps.

#### Heroic notes

- `Rifts of Corruption`
  - Spawn throughout the room.
  - Fire bolts at random players for heavy Shadow damage and 5 Pride.
  - Run over a rift to close it.
  - Closing a rift gives a 1 minute debuff preventing another close.
  - Every player without the debuff should actively look for a rift.
- `Banishment`
  - Banished player is forced to run forward.
  - Hitting walls or Sha mobs kills you.
  - Outside, a copy of the player appears; raid kills it to free them.
  - Inside, collect golden orbs to damage the outside copy faster.
  - Basic path: immediate left, left again, then follow the circle right until released.
- Heroic positioning stays spread; do not use the normal tight-stack plan.
- Assign a surplus of players to each prison.
  - Example 25-man assignment: 5 players per prison so every prison breaks quickly.

### Assignments

- Tanks:
  - Swap before the boss melees a tank with `Wounded Pride`.
  - Off tank picks up `Self Reflection` adds.
- Healers:
  - Track `Gift of the Titans` and `Mark of Arrogance`.
  - Gifted healers dispel marks immediately.
  - Plan CDs for `Swelling Pride` and final burn.
- DPS:
  - Assign interrupts/silences/grips for each `Manifestation of Pride`.
  - Assign prison teams before pull.
  - Heroic: everyone helps close rifts when eligible.

### Common Wipe Causes

- Late `Mark of Arrogance` dispels.
- Players not moving after `Self Reflection` or after `Swelling Pride` swirlies.
- Slow prison breaks.
- Large adds free-casting `Mocking Blast`.
- Projection players missing their green-arrow soak.
- Heroic: rifts left open or banished players hitting walls/mobs.

### Quick Reminders

- Gifted healers dispel marks.
- 35: move, then AoE small adds.
- 65: break all 4 prisons with 3 panel soakers each.
- Kill and interrupt 3 big adds.
- 100: raid CD, move after hit, soak green arrows.
- Heroic: spread, close rifts, kill banishment copies.
