## Malkorok

### Overview

- 25m comp: `2 tanks`, `5 healers` on heroic; normal can add a healer if needed but keep the enrage in mind.
- Two repeating phases:
  - Phase 1: `Ancient Miasma` spread phase; all healing becomes absorb shields.
  - Phase 2: `Blood Rage` stack/split phase; shields drop and healing works normally.
- Main wipe causes:
  - missed `Imploding Energy` soaks.
  - players soaking with weak shields or after touching heroic orbs.
  - bad `Arcing Smash` marker discipline before `Breath of Y'Shaarj`.

#### Heroic notes

- `Essence of Y'Shaarj` orbs spawn constantly at player locations.
  - Avoid them during `Ancient Miasma`; touching one removes your shield and deals health damage you cannot heal back until phase 2.
  - Clear orbs during `Blood Rage` with immunities / strong personals if assigned.
- `Seismic Slam` spawns `Living Corruption`.
  - Tank / taunt / grip it into melee immediately.
  - Do not leave the slow aura on ranged or they will fail `Arcing Smash` / soak movement.

### Phase 1: Ancient Miasma

- Spread evenly around the room so every quadrant can cover `Imploding Energy`.
- Healers keep shields green where possible; players should track their own shield color.
  - Green: safe to soak.
  - Yellow: use a small defensive.
  - Red: ask for help or use a major defensive, but do not leave a soak empty.

- `Imploding Energy`
  - 25m: `7` void zones spawn.
  - One player must soak each within ~4 seconds.
  - Soaking hits the player hard; missed soaks hit the whole raid and are usually fatal.
  - If a zone is uncovered, someone must take it even if they die.

- `Arcing Smash` / `Breath of Y'Shaarj`
  - Mark each of the 3 smash locations.
  - After the third smash, soak the final `Imploding Energy` set first.
  - Then move clear of all 3 marked areas before `Breath of Y'Shaarj`.
  - Remove markers after the breath.

- `Seismic Slam`
  - Spread 5+ yards.
  - Healers rebuild the target's shield quickly.
  - Heroic: bring the spawned `Living Corruption` to melee.

### Phase 2: Blood Rage

- Starts at 100 rage, lasts ~20 seconds.
- Default 25m plan: stack in front of the boss with tanks to split the cleave.
  - Use stacked raid cooldowns such as `Power Word: Barrier`, `Smoke Bomb`, and throughput CDs.
  - End the phase with everyone topped before shields return.
- `Displaced Energy`
  - Debuffed players must not explode in the stack.
  - Normal: run behind / away, call for dispel, then return.
  - Heroic: debuff roots; use snare breaks such as `Stampeding Roar`, `Hand of Freedom`, `Blink`, or `Unbound Will`.
  - Plan two snare-break answers per `Blood Rage`, one for each debuff set.

#### Heroic alternate soak

- A Brewmaster can solo-soak the 25m `Blood Rage` cleave with heavy externals.
  - 25m cleave is roughly `6.5M` total.
  - Requires dedicated healing and chained personals / externals.
  - If using this, the raid can stay spread and `Displaced Energy` is much easier to manage.

### Quick Reminders

- Spread for phase 1; stack for phase 2 unless using the Brewmaster solo-soak plan.
- 25m gets `7` void zones; every one needs a player.
- Mark all 3 `Arcing Smash` zones.
- After third smash: soak voids first, then dodge breath zones.
- Heroic: do not run through orbs while moving to soak.
- Heroic: grip / taunt `Living Corruption` into melee instantly.
