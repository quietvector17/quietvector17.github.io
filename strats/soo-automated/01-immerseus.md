## Immerseus

### Overview

- 25-man setup: 2 tanks, 5-6 healers, balanced DPS with stuns/grips for puddles.
- Fight alternates between DPS phases and split phases.
- Boss does not die at 0 health.
  - Corruption bar starts at `100`.
  - Fight ends when corruption reaches `0`.
- Bloodlust on pull.
- Second pot on the second DPS phase.

### Positioning

- DPS phase:
  - Raid loosely stacks near the entrance with room to sidestep pools.
  - Active tank holds boss off to one side at max safe healing range.
  - Off tank stays with/near the raid and is ready to taunt after `Corrosive Blast`.
- Split phase:
  - Pre-split the raid evenly around the room.
  - Each sector needs DPS and healer coverage.
  - If your sector has no adds, rotate clockwise or help a nearby sector.

### DPS Phase

- Burn boss to 0 health.
- `Corrosive Blast`
  - Frontal cone; one-shots non-tanks.
  - Tank swap every cast.
  - Keep the cone pointed away from the raid.
- Sha pools spawn under players.
  - Keep moving enough to avoid them.
- `Swirl`
  - Boss fires a rotating water beam.
  - Run with/through the boss as needed; do not get clipped.
  - Avoid the extra moving water pools during the cast.

### Split Phase

- Starts when boss reaches 0 health.
- Adds spawn around the edge and move to the center.
- Any add reaching the center explodes for raid damage.
- Kill/heal as many as possible before Immerseus reforms.

#### Sha Puddles

- Hostile adds.
- Kill, stun, grip, and slow them before they reach center.
- Count is based on remaining corruption: 1 hostile puddle per 4 corruption.

#### Contaminated Puddles

- Friendly adds.
- Heal to full.
- They move slower as they are healed.
- When healed to full, nearby players gain a healing buff and mana.
- Count increases as corruption is cleansed.
- Save stronger healing cooldowns for later split phases when more friendly puddles spawn.

#### Heroic notes

- `Swelling Corruption` happens during DPS phase.
  - Boss gains stacks.
  - Each attack removes 1 boss stack, gives the attacker a stacking debuff, and spawns a small add.
  - Stop hitting the boss around 3 stacks; more than 4 is dangerous.
  - AoE spawned adds, then resume boss damage.
- Keep the raid relatively tight during `Swelling Corruption` so the free tank can pick up small adds.
- Tank pattern:
  - Boss tank takes `Corrosive Blast`.
  - Off tank taunts into the same tank spot.
  - Previous tank returns to the raid and picks up small adds.
- During transition, avoid pools being sucked toward the center.
- Center pool grows when adds reach it; heal through it, but do not let it grow by ignoring adds.
- Hybrids help heal during split if they have nothing to damage.

### Assignments

- Tanks:
  - Swap every `Corrosive Blast`.
  - Heroic: non-boss tank gathers small adds from `Swelling Corruption`.
- Healers:
  - Cover assigned split sectors.
  - Plan major raid healing CDs for third split and later.
- DPS:
  - Pre-assign room sectors.
  - Use stuns/grips on distant `Sha Puddles`.
  - Heroic: watch personal `Swelling Corruption` stacks and stop at 3.

### Common Wipe Causes

- Tanks aiming `Corrosive Blast` through the raid.
- Players standing in pools or getting clipped by `Swirl`.
- Poor split-phase coverage leaving sectors without healers.
- Late-phase friendly puddles not healed because cooldowns were spent early.
- Heroic: DPS tunneling past safe `Swelling Corruption` stacks.

### Quick Reminders

- Taunt every `Corrosive Blast`.
- Dodge pools and `Swirl`.
- Split evenly; rotate if your area is empty.
- Kill black puddles, heal friendly puddles.
- Heroic: stop boss at 3 debuff stacks, AoE adds.
