## Garrosh Hellscream

### Overview

- 25m heroic comp: `2-3 tanks`, `3-5 healers`, `17-20 DPS`.
- Long multi-phase fight:
  - Phase 1: Garrosh + Kor'kron adds + Iron Stars.
  - Transition: realm of Y'Shaarj.
  - Phase 2: Garrosh gains corruption abilities and repeats realms.
  - Phase 3: all corruption abilities are empowered.
  - Heroic Phase 4: Ruined Stormwind.
- Main goals:
  - Keep Garrosh energy low by clearing transitions quickly.
  - Control `Touch of Y'Shaarj` immediately.
  - Keep `Minion of Y'Shaarj` deaths separated.
  - Execute heroic `Malice` / `Iron Star` / `Manifest Rage` cleanly.

#### Heroic notes

- Phase 1 `Kor'kron Warbringers` fixate instead of being clean tank targets.
- Transitions are stricter and should be completed fast enough to prevent extra empowered abilities.
  - First transition: aim to keep Garrosh below `25 energy`.
  - Second transition: aim to keep Garrosh below `50 energy`.
  - A third transition usually means the raid is too slow.
- Heroic `Minions of Y'Shaarj` are more dangerous.
  - Do not let them die near each other.
  - Avoid feeding them energy / damage through bad melee handling.
- Killing Phase 3 does not end heroic; Garrosh teleports the raid to Phase 4.

### Phase 1: True Horde

- Tank Garrosh near the center / top of the Horde crest.
- Ranged stack near the entrance on assigned left/right markers.
- `Desecrate`
  - Ranged move as a group when the weapon lands.
  - Kill or DoT weapons enough to keep the ground manageable.
  - Do not bait weapons into melee.
- `Kor'kron Warbringers`
  - Cleave them near Garrosh.
  - Use the Iron Star path to help kill them.
- `Farseer Wolf Rider`
  - Highest add priority.
  - Interrupt `Ancestral Chain Heal`.
  - Avoid unnecessary interrupts on `Chain Lightning` if it causes the rider to become unmanageable.
- `Siege Engineers` / `Iron Star`
  - Assign mobile ranged DPS to kill one engineer.
  - Let the opposite Iron Star roll through adds.
  - Knock adds into the Iron Star path if possible.
  - Everyone stays away from the wall where the Star explodes.
- Push Garrosh to `10%`, then clean up remaining adds before the transition ends.

### Transitions

- Kill all realm adds as quickly as possible.
- Pick up yellow damage-reduction orbs when they are safe.
- Once Garrosh is attackable, stack inside / behind him and dodge `Annihilate` cones.
- Realm handling:
  - Terrace: tanks gather adds and raid AoEs quickly.
  - Jade Serpent: split left/right, then regroup.
  - Red Crane: split left/right for the two large adds.
- The faster the raid reaches Garrosh, the less energy he gains.

### Phase 2: Corruption

- Tank swap on `Gripping Despair` around `8-10` stacks, or earlier if damage is high.
- Keep ranged stacked and move as a group for `Desecrate`.
  - Favor edges / corners so weapons do not consume the middle.
  - Later weapons can often be ignored if placement is clean.
- `Touch of Y'Shaarj`
  - All DPS instantly swap to mind-controlled players.
  - Interrupt every cast.
  - Bring targets low enough to break the MC without killing them.
- `Whirling Corruption`
  - Move far from Garrosh and heal through it.
  - Tanks can drag Garrosh slightly away from the raid.
- `Empowered Whirling Corruption`
  - Dodge impact swirls.
  - Kill spawned `Minions of Y'Shaarj` separated from each other.
  - Do not AoE them down together.

### Phase 3: Empowered Burn

- Garrosh heals to `25%` and uses empowered versions of his Phase 2 abilities.
- Use `Bloodlust` / `Heroism` here unless your plan saves it for Phase 4.
- Keep moving as a group for `Empowered Desecrate`.
  - Weapons cannot be permanently killed.
  - Damage them only if the void zone is blocking movement.
- `Empowered Touch of Y'Shaarj`
  - Targets are immune to loss-of-control effects.
  - Interrupts are mandatory.
  - DPS them out immediately.
- `Empowered Whirling Corruption`
  - Spread enough to dodge missiles.
  - Keep minions separated and kill individually, or have tanks control them if the boss is about to phase.
- Push cleanly into Phase 4 with no MCs, loose minions, or dangerous tank stacks.

### Heroic Phase 4: Ruined Stormwind

- Starts after Phase 3 reaches `0%`.
- Garrosh does RP, teleports the raid, heals to `60%`, and starts at `0 energy`.
- The fight does not pause during RP.
  - Clear leftover MCs.
  - Stabilize tanks.
  - Clean up or control minions.

#### `Malice`

- `15s` debuff on a random non-tank.
- In 25m, each tick must hit `5` nearby players.
- If fewer than 5 players soak a tick, the raid takes damage and Garrosh gains energy.
- Assign rotating Malice soak groups.
  - Soakers take 1 `Malicious Blast` stack, step out until the vulnerability drops, then rotate back if needed.
  - Strong defensives can cover extra stacks.
  - Immunities can save players, but do not rely on immune players as required soak counts.

#### `Bombardment` / Iron Star

- During `Bombardment`, spread and dodge ground impacts.
- After `Bombardment`, intentionally clump exactly one group of at least `7 players` within `8 yards`.
  - This spawns one Iron Star.
  - Avoid accidental extra clumps.
- Assign a mobile kiter to take the Iron Star.
  - Kite around the outside of Stormwind.
  - The Star can refixate to the nearest player, so everyone else stays disciplined.

#### `Manifest Rage`

- At `100 energy`, Garrosh casts `Manifest Rage`.
- Kite the Iron Star into Garrosh during the cast to interrupt / stun him.
- If the Star hits too early, it is wasted.
- If the Star hits too late, `Manifest Rage` spawns adds and can wipe the raid.
- If adds spawn, tanks pick them up and the raid cleaves them, but the priority is still interrupting the cast quickly.

### Quick Reminders

- P1: kill one engineer, let one Iron Star kill adds.
- Interrupt `Ancestral Chain Heal`.
- Transitions: move fast; first below `25 energy`, second below `50 energy`.
- MCs are always top priority.
- Empowered MCs require interrupts; stuns will not work.
- Minions must die separated.
- Phase 4: Malice gets 5 soakers per tick.
- Phase 4: spawn exactly one Iron Star after `Bombardment`.
- Phase 4: hit Garrosh with the Star during `Manifest Rage`.
