## Thok the Bloodthirsty

### Overview

- 25m heroic comp: `2 tanks`, `7 healers`.
- Fight alternates between:
  - Stack phase: hold Thok in place for boss damage.
  - Frenzy / kite phase: kill jailer, open cage, reset into next stack phase.
- Heroic phase order:
  - Normal stack -> Normal kite -> Poison stack -> Poison kite -> Frost stack -> Frost kite -> Fire stack -> Fire kite.
- Main wipe causes:
  - pushing stack phase too early or too late.
  - poor cooldown coverage at high `Acceleration`.
  - fixate path through the raid.
  - bats living too long.
  - missed dispels in poison phases.

#### Heroic notes

- Split into two exact groups on markers, no more than `14` players per group.
  - This prevents accidental `Bloodied` transition in 25m.
  - When ready to transition, collapse the groups together.
- 25m heroic target: usually `26-29` `Deafening Screech` stacks on long stack phases.
  - Do not plan above 30; screeches become lethal.
  - A safer pacing is very high first stack, only ~11-12 on the second, then high again once cooldowns return.
- Plan `Devotion Aura`, `Hand of Protection`, `Spirit Link Totem`, `Smoke Bomb`, `Power Word: Barrier`, throughput CDs, personals, and healthstones.
  - Start personals around 7 stacks.
  - Chain raid CDs from there.
  - Use `Barrier` near the final 3-4 stacks before transition.

### Stack Phase

- Stand on Thok's sides; never in front except tanks, never behind.
- Tanks swap / move for breath stacks.
- Casters must stop casts before `Deafening Screech`.
  - Track Thok energy.
  - Use a stop-casting keybind or macro.

- `Bloodied`
  - 25m transition requires `15` bloodied players within 10 yards.
  - Keep groups split until the planned transition.
  - Collapse only when the raid leader calls for the push.

### Frenzy / Kite Phase

- First fixated player runs immediately toward the entrance.
- Raid moves to the opposite side.
- Kite Thok in a straight line on the side away from the raid.
- Ranged follow Thok for boss damage if safe.
- Tanks / melee kill `Kor'kron Jailer`.
  - Open the cage `2-3` stacks before the desired transition.
  - Use movement help for fixated players at high speed: `Stampeding Roar`, priest feathers, warlock gateways.

### Akolik: Poison Phase

- Free left cage first.
- `Acid Breath` replaces the normal breath and reduces armor.
- `Corrosive Blood` applies dispellable nature DoTs.
- Healers must keep dispels moving in both the stack and following kite phase.
- Heroic: cave bats fly down around the second screech of this stack phase.
  - Offtank picks them up and tanks them in melee for cleave.
  - All DPS hard AoE them.
  - Use second potions and raid CDs here.
  - Aim to have bats dead by ~10 stacks.

### Waterspeaker Gorai: Frost Phase

- `Freezing Breath` can freeze the tank at 5 stacks.
- `Icy Blood` can freeze random players at 5 stacks.
- Break ice tombs quickly.
- Heroic: watch the `Starved Yeti`.
  - It charges between points and can kill anyone in the path.
  - Stack exactly on assigned safe markers.
  - During kite phases, keep looking for the charge marker.
  - Kill or ignore the yeti depending on your group's comfort.

### Warmaster Montak: Fire Phase

- Free back-right cage last.
- Ideally Thok is around `20%` or lower before this phase.
- `Scorching Breath` adds fire damage over time.
- `Burning Blood` drops fire pools under random players.
- 25m heroic plan:
  - Stack on the final marker and chain all remaining cooldowns.
  - Either stand still and heal through low-stack fire, or strafe together left/right as fire spawns.
  - Stay within 10 yards so the final transition happens cleanly.
  - In the last kite phase, ignore the jailer and burn Thok.

### Burn Decisions

- If Thok is `~12%` or lower entering the Frost kite phase, consider ignoring the jailer and killing him in kite.
  - Transitioning again heals him, so do not open another cage if he is already killable.
- If above that, handle jailer and cage normally, then use the fire stack / kite as final burn.

### Quick Reminders

- 25m heroic: two groups, max `14` each, collapse only on call.
- Long stack phases aim `26-29` stacks if cooldowns allow.
- Open cage `2-3` kite stacks before desired reset.
- Ranged follow Thok in kite; melee kill jailer.
- Poison: kill bats fast and dispel constantly.
- Frost: break ice and avoid yeti.
- Fire: chain remaining CDs and finish.
