## Dark Shamans

### Overview

- 25-man normal: 2 tanks, 6-7 healers, favor ranged DPS.
- 25-man heroic: use 3 tanks; this is much easier and is the expected setup.
- Bosses share health and gain abilities at health thresholds.
- Pull bosses outside, kill both wolves before bosses reach 85% if possible.
- Bloodlust at 25% when the bosses cast their own bloodlust.
- This is a control/space fight, not a pure DPS race.

### Normal Positioning

- Kite both bosses together around the central area.
- Move as a group to stay ahead of `Toxic Storm`, tornadoes, `Foul Slime`, `Ashen Wall`, and `Falling Ash`.
- Tanks swap to keep `Froststorm Strike` stacks low.
- Keep room behind the raid for `Foul Geyser` slimes to spawn and be killed before they reach players.

### Earthbreaker Haromm

- `Froststorm Strike`
  - Tank hit that increases frost damage taken.
  - Tank swap to drop stacks.
- At 85%: `Toxic Mist`
  - 25-man applies to 5 players.
  - Nature DoT lasts 30 seconds and ramps hard.
  - Healers prioritize these players; affected players avoid all extra damage.
- At 65%: `Foul Stream`
  - Targeted player moves away from raid and uses a defensive if needed.
  - Everyone else dodges the green line.
- At 45%: `Ashen Wall`
  - Tank moves boss forward after each wall.
  - Do not touch the elementals.

### Wavebinder Kardris

- At 85%: `Toxic Storm`
  - Move away from storm and tornadoes.
  - Watch cast timing; reacting only to the graphic can be late.
- At 65%: `Foul Geyser`
  - Tank runs to spawn slimes away from raid and clumped.
  - Ranged AoE slimes before they reach players.
- At 45%: `Falling Ash`
  - Move out of the large circle.
  - Use raid cooldown shortly before the meteor impact.

#### Heroic notes

- Each boss gains a 95% ability:
  - Haromm: `Iron Tomb`.
  - Kardris: `Iron Prison`.
- Split bosses far enough apart so abilities only affect the nearest group.
- Haromm group on 25-man:
  - 2 tanks, 3 healers, all melee DPS.
  - Move Haromm toward the Drag entrance.
  - Healers hug the wall to drop `Iron Tomb` at the edge.
  - Tank keeps boss back to wall and moves forward after each `Ashen Wall`.
  - `Foul Stream` targets should stand still; everyone else moves away.
  - Players with `Toxic Mist` must not be hit by `Foul Stream` or other nature effects.
- Kardris group on 25-man:
  - 1 tank, remaining healers, all ranged DPS.
  - Kite around the main room, away from storms and tornadoes.
  - Mark the tank so ranged know where `Foul Geyser` slimes will spawn.
  - `Iron Prison` targets need full health plus absorb/personal/external just before it expires.
  - Use a raid cooldown for each `Falling Ash`; warn the Haromm group so they use personals if out of range.

### Common Wipe Causes

- Wolves live into 85% abilities.
- `Toxic Mist` players take avoidable nature damage.
- `Iron Tomb` / `Ashen Wall` placements consume all usable space.
- `Iron Prison` expires without an absorb or cooldown.
- Kardris tank spawns slimes in the raid.
- Raid misses `Falling Ash` impact cooldowns.

### Quick Reminders

- Kill wolves first.
- Normal: kite together in a circle.
- Heroic: 3 tanks, split Haromm melee group from Kardris ranged group.
- 25-man `Toxic Mist`: 5 targets.
- Use cooldowns for `Iron Prison` and `Falling Ash`.
- Bloodlust at 25%.
