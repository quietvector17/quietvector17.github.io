## Iron Juggernaut

### Overview

- 25-man: use 2 tanks and 6-7 healers; extra healers are fine because the DPS check is light.
- Two repeating modes:
  - `Assault Mode`: boss is tanked normally.
  - `Siege Mode`: boss roots, raid handles knockbacks, mines, tar, and lasers.
- Bloodlust on pull, especially on heroic.
- Primary raid rules: stay spread 6 yards, soak mines, dodge ground effects, never kite laser through tar.

### Positioning

- Tank boss near an enclosed wall/terrain area and face him away from the raid.
- Raid spreads loosely on the boss's side, within healing range.
- Before `Siege Mode`, everyone positions between the boss and nearby terrain so `Shock Pulse` knocks into a wall instead of across the room.
- Keep enough spread for `Demolisher Cannons`, but do not outrange healers.

### Assault Mode

- Tanks:
  - Swap after each `Flame Vents` to reset `Ignite Armor` stacks.
  - Face the frontal cone away from the raid.
- Raid:
  - Stay 6 yards apart for `Demolisher Cannons` splash.
  - Move from `Mortar Cannons` circles; use a personal if targeted in the center and low.
  - Move out of `Borer Drill` lines or be ready for healing.
- `Crawler Mines`
  - 25-man spawns 5 mines.
  - Assign tanks/immunities/strong defensives to click mines before `Detonation Sequence` finishes.
  - On heroic, aim to soak every mine; do not intentionally let raid explosions through.
  - Off-tank should call quickly if they cannot reach one.

#### Heroic notes

- `Ricochet`
  - Saw blade jumps through players for heavy damage and knockback.
  - Stay spread and avoid if possible, but do not tunnel so hard that mines/ground effects are missed.
- Mine soak damage is about 1.5M physical before armor; tanks take much less with mitigation.

### Siege Mode

- Boss stops meleeing and roots.
- Continues using `Crawler Mines` and `Demolisher Cannons`.
- Raid takes constant nature damage; healers plan throughput.
- `Shock Pulse`
  - Everyone must be knocked into a wall/terrain.
  - Use a defensive raid cooldown during the pulse and healing cooldowns after.
- Tar puddles:
  - Move out; they slow and add damage.
  - Keep paths clear for laser kiting.
- Laser fixate:
  - Fixated player kites the laser around the room.
  - Never let the laser touch tar or it explodes on the raid.

#### Heroic notes

- `Mortar Barrage`
  - Many large circles during `Siege Mode`.
  - Dodging is the priority; getting hit can kill you.
- Good DPS usually means only one `Siege Mode`, but keep assignments ready for a repeat.

### Quick Reminders

- Spread 6 yards all fight.
- Tanks swap after `Flame Vents`.
- 25-man mines: 5 spawn; soak all with tanks/immunities/defensives.
- Siege: backs to wall for `Shock Pulse`.
- Laser never crosses tar.
- Heroic: dodge `Mortar Barrage`; use raid CD for each `Shock Pulse`.
