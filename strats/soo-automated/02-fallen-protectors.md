## Fallen Protectors

### Overview

- 25-man setup: 2 tanks, 5-6 healers, strong cleave/multidot is valuable.
- Kill all 3 bosses at the same time.
- Each boss triggers `Desperate Measures` at `66%` and `33%`.
- Never push two bosses into `Desperate Measures` together.
- Keep final health even after all bosses are below `33%`.

### Positioning

- Raid loosely spreads near center.
- Tank `Rook Stonetoe` slightly to the side and face him away from the raid.
- Tank `He Softfoot` where poison can be kited around the raid edge.
- `Sun Tenderheart` does not need to be tanked.
- Collapse into Sun's bubble during her `Desperate Measures`.

### Phase Order

- Normal pacing:
  - Push `Rook Stonetoe` to `66%`.
  - Push `He Softfoot` to `66%`.
  - Push `Sun Tenderheart` to `66%`.
  - Repeat for `33%`.
  - Finish all 3 together.
- Heroic pacing:
  - Push `Rook Stonetoe` first.
  - Push `Sun Tenderheart` next, but wait until after Rook uses `Spinning Crane Kick`.
  - Push `He Softfoot` after Sun.
  - Repeat the same cycle.
  - Bloodlust during the second cycle between `66%` and `33%`.

### Rook Stonetoe

- `Vengeful Strikes` / frontal cone:
  - Face away from raid.
- `Corrupted Brew`
  - Move out of green swirls.
  - Heroic: each cast travels faster; later casts become difficult to dodge.
- `Spinning Crane Kick`
  - Rook charges a player and spins.
  - Move out immediately.
  - Dispel the post-kick DoT if someone is clipped.

#### Desperate Measures: Rook

- Rook disappears and spawns `Sorrow`, `Misery`, and `Gloom`.
- Normal:
  - Kill `Sorrow` first.
  - Stack in melee for `Inferno Blast` unless assigned to immunity-soak.
  - Assign interrupts on `Gloom` for `Corruption Shock`.
  - Tank `Misery`; give that tank extra healing.
- Heroic:
  - All 3 adds share health.
  - Stack on `Embodied Sorrow` spawn marker immediately.
  - Multidot/cleave all 3 adds.
  - Pull or silence `Gloom` into the stack and keep it interrupted.
  - `He Softfoot` tank stays out with `Misery`.
  - Watch for `Noxious Poison`, `Corrupted Brew`, and `Calamity` during this phase.

### He Softfoot

- `Garrote`
  - Persistent raid DoT; heal through it.
  - It drops when He enters `Desperate Measures`, so pushing He can relieve high raid bleed damage.
- `Gouge`
  - Tank turns away before cast finishes to avoid stun and aggro drop.
- `Instant Poison`
  - Extra tank damage.
- `Noxious Poison`
  - Tank kites He around the edge of the raid.
  - Raid avoids poison trails.

#### Desperate Measures: He

- He disappears and spawns `Embodied Anguish`.
- `Mark of Anguish`
  - Fixates the marked player and deals stacking Shadow damage.
  - Use personals while holding it.
  - Transfer with Extra Action Button before stacks/cooldowns become unsafe.
- Normal:
  - Pass `Mark of Anguish` to a tank and avoid unnecessary transfers.
- Heroic:
  - Cannot pass the mark to tanks.
  - Pre-assign immunity/avoidance holders.
  - Good targets: rogues with `Evasion`, mages with `Ice Block`, hunters with `Deterrence`, warlocks with `Dark Bargain`, warriors with `Die by the Sword`.
  - Call the next target before passing.

### Sun Tenderheart

- `Mind Sear`
  - Target moves away from others.
  - Interruptible, but she will recast on another player.
- `Shadow Word: Bane`
  - Dispel before it ticks and spreads.
  - Use Mass Dispel or healer callouts.
  - Healers must track it clearly on raid frames.
- `Calamity`
  - Raid hit based on max HP.
  - Top the raid before it lands; low players use personals.
  - Heroic: starts at 30% max HP and increases by 10% per cast; plan raid CDs for later casts.

#### Desperate Measures: Sun

- Room goes dark; stack in the bubble for damage reduction.
- Kill the two large adds to end the phase.
- Tank the large adds near the bubble so melee can cleave without leaving the damage reduction.
- Cleave/AoE small adds because damage copies to the large adds.
- Chain raid healing cooldowns.
- Ranged focus large adds from inside the bubble; melee cleave small adds.
- Heroic: watch floor effects while stacked in the bubble.

### Assignments

- Tanks:
  - One tank on Rook, one on He.
  - He tank handles `Gouge` turns and poison kiting.
  - Rook tank faces Rook away from raid.
- Healers:
  - Fast dispels on `Shadow Word: Bane`.
  - Raid CDs for Sun `Desperate Measures` and later heroic `Calamity` casts.
- DPS:
  - Control pushes at `66%` and `33%`.
  - Swap immediately to `Desperate Measures` adds.
  - Assign `Gloom` interrupts and heroic `Mark of Anguish` pass targets.

### Common Wipe Causes

- Pushing multiple bosses to `66%` / `33%` together.
- Rook facing the raid.
- `Shadow Word: Bane` dispels late, allowing spreads.
- `Inferno Blast` not stacked or `Gloom` not interrupted.
- Heroic: Sun pushed while Rook is about to `Spinning Crane Kick` into the bubble.
- Heroic: unclear `Mark of Anguish` pass order.

### Quick Reminders

- Push one boss at a time.
- Kill all 3 together.
- Dispel `Shadow Word: Bane` instantly.
- Stack for Rook `Inferno Blast` and Sun bubble.
- Heroic order: Rook, Sun after kick, He.
