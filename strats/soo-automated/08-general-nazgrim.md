## General Nazgrim

### Overview

- 25-man normal: 3 tanks is advised to keep `Sundering Blow` rage low.
- 25-man heroic: use 3 tanks and 5 healers.
- Add waves of 2 spawn every 45 seconds.
- Boss stances rotate every minute:
  - `Battle Stance`
  - `Berserker Stance`
  - `Defensive Stance`
- Fight is about rage control and add priority.

### Positioning

- Tank Nazgrim near the entrance/side wall so banners are easy to swap to.
- Tank adds away from the boss, especially away from `Healing Tide Totem` range.
- Off-tanks pick up `Kor'kron Ironblade`s and keep them out of the raid.
- Ranged stay spread enough for `Heroic Shockwave` and add mechanics.

### Boss Rage

- Nazgrim uses the most expensive rage ability he can afford every 15 seconds.
- Prevent rage gain by:
  - Killing `Kor'kron Banner` instantly.
  - Dodging `Heroic Shockwave` and fire lines.
  - Keeping tank `Sundering Blow` stacks low.
  - Not attacking during `Defensive Stance`.
- Rage abilities:
  - 30 rage: `Heroic Shockwave`; spread and dodge the lines.
  - 50 rage: `Kor'kron Banner`; top priority.
  - 70 rage: `War Song`; raid cooldown and personals if low.
  - 100 rage: `Ravager`; avoid reaching this.

### Stances

- `Battle Stance`
  - Boss gains 1 rage per second.
  - Kill priority adds and controlled boss damage.
- `Berserker Stance`
  - Boss deals and takes 25% more damage.
  - Push boss damage hard after high-priority adds are handled.
  - Heroic: still kill `Arcweaver`, `War Shaman`, and `Assassin`; ignore `Sniper` and `Ironblade` until after Berserker if needed.
  - Heroic: use bloodlust on the second `Berserker Stance`.
- `Defensive Stance`
  - Boss takes 25% less damage and attacks against him generate rage.
  - Stop boss DPS before the stance starts.
  - Pets can stay on boss.
  - Kill adds during this window.

### Add Priority

- Normal priority:
  - `Kor'kron Banner`
  - `Healing Tide Totem`
  - `Kor'kron Warshaman`
  - `Kor'kron Arcweaver`
  - `Kor'kron Assassin`
  - `Kor'kron Ironblade`
- Heroic priority:
  - `Kor'kron Banner`
  - `Healing Tide Totem`
  - `Kor'kron Arcweaver`
  - `Kor'kron Warshaman`
  - `Kor'kron Assassin`
  - `Kor'kron Sniper`
  - `Kor'kron Ironblade`

### Add Handling

- `Kor'kron Banner`
  - Spawns next to Nazgrim.
  - Tank calls spawn; DPS use target macro if available.
- `Healing Tide Totem`
  - Move mobs away immediately, then kill the totem.
  - Do not stun mobs on top of the totem.
- `Warshaman`
  - Interrupt `Chain Heal`.
  - Dispel `Earth Shield`.
  - Move when totems drop.
- `Arcweaver`
  - Stun rotation while killing.
  - Kill before `Arcane Shock` damage ramps.
- `Assassin`
  - Hunter `Flare` to reveal early.
  - Fixated player faces/kites it; never let it `Backstab`.
  - Slow, root, stun, and kill after higher priorities.
- `Ironblade`
  - Tanks pick up and avoid standing in `Ironstorm`.
  - Kill mostly during `Defensive Stance`.

#### Heroic notes

- `Kor'kron Sniper`
  - Fixates a player with `Hunter's Mark`.
  - Fixated player turns the sniper toward the nearest wall.
  - Cone is 40 yards and hits very hard; never face it through the raid.
  - Kill after assassins unless Berserker damage timing says to leave it briefly.
- At 10%, ignore adds and burn Nazgrim.
  - Keep any active `Warshaman` interrupted and away from boss so he is not healed.
- If `Ravager`s spawn, the cause is usually slow banners, shockwave hits, or tank stacks too high.

### Quick Reminders

- 25-man heroic: 3 tanks, 5 healers.
- Banners and totems are immediate swaps.
- Stop boss DPS in `Defensive Stance`.
- Pump boss in `Berserker Stance`.
- Heroic sniper target faces wall.
- Burn boss at 10%.
