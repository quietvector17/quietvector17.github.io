## Paragons of the Klaxxi

### Overview

- 25m heroic comp: `2 tanks`, `5 healers`.
- Council fight with `9` bosses total; `3` active at a time.
- On normal, order changes weekly; on heroic, order is fixed.
- Do not spread cleave randomly:
  - Active Paragons heal to full when one dies.
  - Active Paragons gain damage over time / on deaths, so slow kills snowball.
- Main wipe causes:
  - missed active-mitigation checks.
  - poor `Aim` soaking.
  - uncontrolled bloods / parasites.
  - bad `Fiery Edge` spacing.
  - failed `Catalyst` handling.
  - deaths to `Mesmerize`, `Death From Above`, or `Reave`.

#### Heroic notes

- Heroic kill plan from transcript:
  - `Skeer` -> `Rik'kal` -> `Korven` -> `Hisek` -> `Xaril` -> `Iyyokuk` -> `Kaz'tik` -> `Kil'ruk` -> `Ka'roz`.
- Use `Bloodlust` and second potions when `Xaril` lands.
  - Goal: kill him before his second `Catalyst`.
- 25m should spread broadly around the room for `Fiery Edge`, with healers assigned to cover specific areas.

### Opening: Skeer / Rik'kal / Hisek

- Start by DPSing `Rik'kal`.
- When first `Bloodletting` spawns, switch all DPS to `Skeer`.
  - If `Rik'kal` is lowest health, bloods travel to him instead of Skeer.
  - Tank `Rik'kal` ~15 yards away because bloods do AoE healing when they arrive.
- Ignore first blood set if it is safely going to `Rik'kal`.
- Second blood set:
  - Assign slows and 2-3 stuns per blood.
  - Use strongest stuns first, weaker later for DR.
  - Kill `Skeer` before bloods reach him.

### Rik'kal / Parasites

- Normal: tanks use active mitigation during `Injection` so parasites do not spawn.
- Heroic: 25m needs parasite spawns so transformed scorpions can eat.
  - Let one injection through early, then use active mitigation for later casts.
  - 25m gets `8` parasites per injection and `3` scorpions per mutation.
  - Plan for a second parasite set shortly after `Skeer` dies.
  - Four scorpions need to eat twice so no parasites remain later.
- Parasites must be crowd controlled until eaten.
  - They fixate and cast `Feed` from range for heavy damage.
- Scorpions use `Prey` on button 4 by default to eat parasites.
  - Eat quickly; the button can come off cooldown before transformation ends.
- Kill `Rik'kal` ideally after the fourth mutation.

### Hisek / Aim

- `Aim`
  - Target is knocked back and stunned; yellow beam shows the shot.
  - 25m heroic: assign `5` soakers plus the target if possible.
  - Strong soakers: rogues, tanks, warlocks, mages, players with strong personals.
  - Soakers stand in the line but remain 5 yards apart.
  - Use an external / healer CD on the target if needed.
- `Rapid Fire`
  - Bolts spread outward and can kill later in the fight.
  - Melee run out during the cast; everyone dodges gaps.
- `Multi-Shot`
  - Unavoidable burst; heal through it.

### Ka'roz

- Lands after `Skeer` dies while `Rik'kal` is being finished.
- `Whirling` charges several players.
  - Move out of the charged line before the next charge or you get stunned and take heavy damage.
- `Hurl Amber`
  - Move before the pool lands; even brief contact can kill on heroic.
- Keep him controlled while killing higher-priority targets.

### Korven / Amber

- After `Rik'kal` dies, split DPS between `Korven` and `Hisek`.
- Heroic amber cannot be killed.
  - Push `Hisek` to 50% when `Korven` is around 55%.
  - `Hisek` gets ambered.
  - Swap all DPS to `Korven` and kill him before he can amber again.
- Tanks handle `Shield Bash` / `Vicious Assault`.
  - Offtank taunts when tank is stunned.
  - Face the cone away from the stunned tank and melee.

### Iyyokuk / Fiery Edge

- After `Korven` dies, kill `Hisek` while handling `Iyyokuk`.
- 25m: spread evenly around the room.
  - Assign healers to zones so every area has coverage.
- `Fiery Edge`
  - Players with matching traits create fire lines.
  - Pre-spread using boss mod/chat bubble warnings.
  - Distance between linked players reduces damage.
  - Once lines appear, avoid dragging them through others unless you must dodge.
  - Use at least one raid CD for each `Fiery Edge`.
- `Diminish`
  - Tank damage that can execute below 25%; keep tanks stable.

### Xaril / Catalyst

- Kill `Xaril` immediately when he lands.
- Use `Bloodlust`, second potions, and cooldowns.
- Goal: kill before second `Catalyst`.
- Heroic catalyst colors:
  - `Orange Catalyst`: red/yellow players create expanding orange rings.
    - Red/yellow run to the edge as the cast starts.
  - `Purple Catalyst`: red/blue players are forced forward and drop fire.
    - Turn in place without moving forward; one second before it ends, move out.
  - `Green Catalyst`: yellow/blue players drop moving green pools.
    - Avoid pools for the rest of the fight.

### Kaz'tik / Mesmerize

- After `Xaril`, 25m usually kills `Iyyokuk` before `Kaz'tik`.
- Stay away from hungry kunchongs.
- `Mesmerize`
  - Ranged swap to the active kunchong and burn 30% health to break the pull.
  - Use `Life Grip` or tank `Encasing Amber` from Korven's corpse to stop the player if needed.
  - The same kunchong can cast multiple `Mesmerize` in a row.
  - If it is too high energy on the second cast, let it mature and have a tank pick it up.
- Mature kunchongs hit tanks very hard.
  - Use tank CDs and kill quickly.
  - In an emergency they can be kited briefly.

### Kil'ruk / Ka'roz Finish

- When `Kil'ruk` lands, finish whichever of `Iyyokuk` / `Kaz'tik` remains.
- Stay spread for `Death From Above`.
  - Run as soon as he casts; the red circle can one-shot with stacks.
- `Reave`
  - Pulls players toward him and deals raid damage.
  - Damage is worse closer to him; run away until the cast ends.
- Kill `Kil'ruk`, then finish `Ka'roz`.
  - If `Ka'roz` enrages, use all remaining tank cooldowns.
  - Keep avoiding `Whirling` and amber pools while burning.

### Corpse Abilities

- Assign useful corpse abilities before pull.
- `Korven`: tank can `Encasing Amber` a player for damage immunity / `Mesmerize` control.
- `Hisek`: DPS `Snipe` increases physical damage taken by the target.
- `Rik'kal`: scorpion form can kill parasites with `Prey`.
- `Ka'roz`: balcony `Hurl Amber` can help kill amber targets on normal.
- `Skeer`: DPS attacks drop healing spheres.
- `Xaril` / `Iyyokuk`: healer throughput buttons.

### Quick Reminders

- Opening: damage `Rik'kal`, switch to `Skeer` when bloods spawn.
- 25m heroic: feed every scorpion; do not leave parasites alive late.
- `Aim`: assigned 5 soakers, 5 yards apart.
- Push `Hisek` to 50% to bait Korven amber, then kill `Korven`.
- Use raid CDs for every `Fiery Edge`.
- `Bloodlust` on `Xaril`; kill before second `Catalyst`.
- Break `Mesmerize` fast or control the player.
- Run from `Death From Above` and away during `Reave`.
