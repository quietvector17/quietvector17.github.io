## Siegecrafter Blackfuse

### Overview

- 25m heroic comp: `2 tanks`, `4-5 healers`.
- Make 2 conveyor teams of `5 DPS` and alternate every assembly line.
- Fight priorities:
  - Conveyor team kills the assigned weapon every wave.
  - Ranged control sawblade / laser placement.
  - Raid kills `Crawler Mines` quickly with slows and stuns.
  - Tanks kill `Automated Shredders` before `Overload` stacks snowball.

#### Heroic notes

- Each assembly line leaves two weapons active:
  - one normal.
  - one overcharged.
- Blackfuse generally overcharges by priority:
  - `Electromagnet` -> `Crawler Mines` -> `Shockwave Missile Turret` -> `Laser Turret`.
  - He usually avoids overcharging the same available weapon twice in a row.
- Belt failure, loose mines, and bad laser placement are the main wipe causes.
- Do not send extra belt DPS unless replacing a death; boss enrage matters.

### Conveyor Belt

- 25m: 2 teams of 5 DPS.
- Best belt classes:
  - Rogues are excellent; save cooldowns for every belt and use defensives for `Superheated`.
  - Ret paladins and hunters are strong.
  - Mobile melee are generally preferred; keep ranged on platform for mine control.
- Heroic belt fire moves right-to-left.
  - Learn the timing and move through decisively.
  - Hesitating often causes missed weapon kills.
- Use a warlock gateway from blue marker / pipe edge if available.
  - Belt players return faster and gain more boss uptime.

### Normal Weapon Handling

- `Crawler Mines`
  - Slow, root, stun, knock back, and kill before they reach targets.
  - Default normal-mode disable priority was mines first, then magnet if no mines.
- `Electromagnet`
  - Pulls players and drags sawblades across the room.
  - Never stand to the right of a sawblade during magnet.
- `Laser Turret`
  - Fixated player kites beam without cutting through the raid.
  - Do not stand in fire.
- `Shockwave Missile Turret`
  - Dodge ripple rings; step into already-hit sections.

### Overcharged Weapons

- `Crawler Mines`
  - Large mine jumps down; killing it spawns 2 smaller mines.
  - Requires boss cleave, strong AoE slows, stuns, knockbacks, and fast target swaps.
- `Electromagnet`
  - Pushes and pulls players repeatedly.
  - Sawblades are thrown back and forth; bad sawblade placement becomes lethal.
- `Laser Turret`
  - Spawns 3 fixed fire rings with gaps.
  - Correct positioning makes them avoidable.
- `Shockwave Missile Turret`
  - Spawns 3 missiles with health bars.
  - All DPS swap and kill each missile while dodging ripples.

### Positioning Plan

- Start ranged near center.
- Drop the first `Launch Sawblade` in the center for the first `Automated Shredder`.
- After first sawblade, ranged move to green.
  - Warlocks may stay slightly short so gateways do not despawn.
  - Nobody should be on red yet.
- First two laser fixates:
  - Laser target runs directly back or left, never right.
  - Raid steps right out of fire.
  - After second laser, ranged stack on red.
- General rule:
  - Drop sawblades to the left of the raid.
  - Raid inches right as needed.
  - Stay stacked on one person so there is always space left for the next blade.
  - Lasers go left or directly back; never right.

### Heroic Wave Plan

- Wave 1: overcharged mines + normal laser.
  - First laser runs back; raid steps right.
  - After second laser, stack red.
  - Tank boss on mine spawn and cleave mines hard.
- Wave 2: overcharged laser + normal mines.
  - If positioned correctly, fire rings miss the raid.
  - Ranged kill normal mines to the right.
- Wave 3: overcharged magnet + normal laser.
  - Wait for laser under raid, target runs back through inner circle.
  - Raid moves right to blue; use `Power Word: Barrier` or equivalent.
  - When empowered fire despawns, move to edge.
  - During magnet, sawblade targets drop left; laser targets run back.
- Wave 4: normal laser + overcharged mines.
  - Wait for first laser, move back toward red.
  - Boss on left-side mine spawn; AoE with slows/stuns.
  - After mines die, move toward yellow.
- Wave 5: overcharged magnet + normal mines.
  - Pre-slow mines near purple.
  - Kill as they move through melee.
  - When magnet ends, run back to red.
- Wave 6: overcharged laser + normal mines.
  - Kill mines, stay stacked, keep dropping blades left and twitching right.
- Wave 7: normal laser + overcharged mines.
  - Laser target left/back; raid right.
  - Tank boss on close mine spawn and use every stun needed.
- Wave 8: overcharged magnet + normal mines.
  - Move to safe edge and kill mines as they pass melee.
- Wave 9: normal mines + overcharged laser.
  - After magnet, immediately return red.
  - Tank boss near mines; kill them.
- Wave 10: normal laser + overcharged mines.
  - Focus fully on mines; keep blades left.
- Wave 11: normal magnet + overcharged missile.
  - Run yellow for magnet.
  - All DPS kill each of the 3 missiles.
  - Use remaining raid and personal CDs.
- Wave 12+: kill boss.
  - Stay together on the right.
  - Handle normal mines / overcharged magnet if needed.
  - Boss should die before death-dealer turrets begin killing players.

### Shredders / Tanking

- Normal plan: tank `Automated Shredders` 35-40 yards from Blackfuse so they are not healed.
- Heroic high-stack plan:
  - Let tanks build `Electrostatic Charge` to ~7-8 stacks once stable.
  - Shredders can be killed near the boss because tank damage outpaces healing.
  - Start with 1 tank on boss; swap when first shredder spawns.
  - Use center sawblade to help kill first shredder.
  - Tank returns and taunts before stacks fall off.
  - Build to ~5 stacks before next shredder, then alternate around 7 stacks.
- Move shredder slightly out before `Death From Above` so melee are not hit.
- If a shredder lives too long, call DPS help and use ground effects / sawblades.

### Quick Reminders

- 25m belt teams are `5 DPS` each.
- First sawblade goes center.
- Ranged move green, then red after 2 lasers.
- Drop sawblades left; raid steps right.
- Lasers go left or back, never right.
- Tank boss on mines whenever possible.
- For overcharged magnet, move to the planned safe edge.
- Wave 11: all DPS kill overcharged missiles.
