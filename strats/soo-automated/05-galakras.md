## Galakras

### Overview

- 25-man: use 2 tanks, 6 healers, and assign tower/hill teams before pull.
- Fight flow:
  - Clear ground waves and protect friendly NPCs.
  - Kill miniboss + `Demolisher`, clear South tower.
  - Defend North tower while South team returns.
  - Kill second miniboss + `Demolisher`, clear North tower.
  - Fire both tower turrets at the boss at the same time.
  - Stack/control `Flames of Galakrond` and burn the boss.

### Ground Waves

- Priority:
  - `War Banner` / `Healing Tide Totem`
  - `Tidal Shaman`
  - `Bonecrusher`
  - Miniboss targets
  - Other adds
- `Tidal Shaman`
  - Focus and interrupt every cast possible, especially heals.
  - Kill `Healing Tide Totem` instantly.
- `Flag Bearer`
  - Kill `War Banner` instantly; it buffs all adds heavily.
- `Bonecrusher`
  - Stun, interrupt, or taunt `Fracture` when it charges friendly NPCs.
  - Letting NPCs die is a common wipe cause.
- `Dragonmaw Ebon Stalker`
  - Move out of gray `Shadow Assault` cones.
- `Korgra the Snake`
  - Kill stalkers first, then burn Korgra fully before swapping.
  - Move out of poison clouds.
  - Burn harder after 50% when `Poison Bolt Volley` starts.

### Towers

- Kill each `Demolisher` before sending players into the tower.
  - While a `Demolisher` is alive, tower players take heavy damage and stuns.
- South tower:
  - Send 1 tank, 5 DPS, 2 healers on 25-man.
  - Clear all tower mobs quickly.
  - Avoid the tower miniboss cone; it can knock players off.
  - One player stays in the turret to shoot down proto-drakes, then drops the rope.
- North tower:
  - Start defending North hill immediately after South tower starts.
  - Send 1-2 ranged and 1 healer to hold North until South team returns.
  - Kill `Tidal Shaman`, avoid the miniboss frontal/`Skull Cracker`, kill `Demolisher`, then climb.
- Turrets:
  - When North tower is nearly clear, send one player back up South tower by rope.
  - Both turret players shoot Galakras at the same time to force the landing.

#### Heroic notes

- Towers do not just become safe; protect demolition crews opening the doors.
- South hill opener on 25-man:
  - 3 ranged DPS, 2 healers, optionally the tower tank.
  - Kill grunts attacking the crew with stuns, knockbacks, and slows.
  - Help the ground raid from range when no grunts are up.
- South tower team on 25-man: 1 tank, 5 DPS, 2 healers.
- After South team climbs, assign 1-2 ranged and 1 healer to North hill immediately.
- Common heroic wipes:
  - Grunts kill demolition crew.
  - `Tidal Shaman` heals get through.
  - `Bonecrusher` `Fracture` kills NPCs.
  - Proto-drakes are not cleared by turret.

### Boss Ground Phase

- Tanks:
  - Face boss away from raid.
  - Swap for `Flames of Galakrond` DoT stacks.
  - Off-tank stands to the side so fireballs do not refresh stacks.
- Raid:
  - Split into two melee-range stacks, one on each hind leg.
  - One caller assigns each `Flames of Galakrond` target to run behind Red or Yellow.
  - Fireball must pass through a group to reduce final explosion damage.
  - Alternate groups so stacks can reset.
- Use raid cooldowns during the 7 fire pulses, especially as the boss gains fire damage stacks.
- Burn before permanent fire damage stacks become unhealable.

### Quick Reminders

- Banners and healing totems die immediately.
- Interrupt shamans; stop `Fracture`.
- Kill `Demolisher` before tower.
- Turrets must shoot boss together.
- Ground phase: two stacks, called fireball soaks, cooldown fire pulses.
