## Spoils of Pandaria

### Overview

- 25m comp: `2 tanks`, `5 healers` on heroic; split into two balanced groups.
- Each side clears two rooms, one `Mogu` and one `Mantid`.
- Kill crate mobs to fill `Titan Energy`.
  - Small crates: `1` energy.
  - Medium crates: `3` energy.
  - Large crates: `14` energy.
  - Pandaren crates give no energy but give role buffs.
- Each room has ~4.5 minutes before it explodes.
- Use separate voice channels if possible; each room needs independent calls for crate pacing and sparks.
- Do not click the lever until both groups are ready to swap sides; uneven timing can strand a weak group.

#### Heroic notes

- Every time one room kills an add, an `Unstable Spark` spawns in the other room.
  - Spark starts a 10 second cast.
  - If not killed, it explodes for ~500K raid damage.
  - Assign 1 player per room to call sparks.
  - With healer Pandaren buff, healers face sparks and often kill them with tidal waves.
  - Without healer buff, assign 1-2 ranged to hard swap every spark.
- Heroic room plan is generally: `2 Pandaren` -> `1 large + 2 small` -> all remaining smalls/mediums.
- `Bloodlust` in Mogu when opening the large crate.

### Pandaren Crates

- Open Pandaren crates first in each room.
  - Open first, DPS it to ~40%, then open second.
  - Kill first, then second, and click buffs immediately.
- Stay spread while Pandaren mobs are active.
  - `Brewmaster` keg splash and breath combos can kill stacked players.
  - Breath is aimed at the tank; melee dodge early or use personals.

- `Windwalker`
  - Charges and leaves damaging blossoms.
  - DPS buff: chance for bonus fire damage.
- `Mistweaver`
  - `Gusting Crane Kick` pulls players and damages within 10 yards.
  - Healer buff: healing can fire tidal waves that heal allies and damage enemies.
  - Healers should stand so waves hit sparks and mobs.
- `Brewmaster`
  - Rotating breath and keg slows.
  - Tank buff: attacks can deal large AoE damage and stun.

### Mogu Rooms

#### Heroic chest order

- 2 Pandaren crates.
- 1 large crate + up to 2 small crates.
- Clear smalls and mediums clockwise while respecting priority mobs.
- Do not open a second `Anima Golem` unless the first is about to die.
- Slow down small openings while a `Burial Urn` is alive.

#### Mogu priorities

- `Burial Urn`
  - Highest small-crate priority.
  - Spawns `Sparks of Life`; focus immediately so the room does not snowball.
- `Anima Golem`
  - Highest medium-crate priority.
  - Tanks move mobs out of red healing pools instantly.
  - Players must soak each red beam / pillar.
  - If alive too long, beam count increases and the room becomes unstable.
- `Shadow Ritualist`
  - Interrupt `Forbidden Magic`.
  - Dispel the stacking debuff when damage is high.
  - Move mobs out of `Rune of Power`.
- Large Mogu
  - Heavy raid AoE while active.
  - Kill statues as they spawn; each statue buffs damage by 10%.
  - Use raid CDs during major casts, especially on heroic.
  - Tanks should face large mobs away from the group and move them out of bad ground immediately.

### Mantid Rooms

#### Heroic chest order

- 2 Pandaren crates.
- 1 large crate + 2 adjacent small crates.
- Keep 2-3 smalls alive while the large is up.
- After the large dies, clear remaining smalls and mediums clockwise.
- Two mediums or 3-4 smalls are usually manageable, but slow down for priority mobs.

#### Mantid priorities

- `Amber-Encased Kunchong`
  - Highest priority in the room.
  - Stop opening aggressively until it dies.
  - Move out of pheromone pools.
- `Set'thik Wind Wielder`
  - Highest medium priority.
  - Focus it over `Amber Priest`.
  - Move away from tornado paths; tanks avoid dragging melee through them.
  - Offensive dispel its buff quickly.
- `Kor'thik Warcaller`
  - Dispel enrage immediately with rogue / hunter tools if available.
  - If enrage stays up, tank kites or uses cooldowns.
- `Set'thik Bombardier`
  - Spread for splash.
  - Move out of `Gusting Bomb` lines.
- `Mantid Commander`
  - Heavy raid AoE while active.
  - `Set to Blow`: move to the side and use extra action button until all stacks are gone.
  - Drop bombs in already-cleared space; do not block future movement.
  - Immunity classes can sometimes soak the debuff away from the raid instead of filling the room with bombs.
  - Keep opening slower while bombs or pheromone pools are consuming safe floor space.

### Quick Reminders

- Balance AoE and single-target DPS in both groups.
- Pandaren first; healer buff is huge on heroic.
- Heroic: kill every `Unstable Spark` before the cast finishes.
- Mogu: `Burial Urn` -> `Anima Golem`; soak beams and move from pools.
- Mantid: `Kunchong` -> `Wind Wielder`; dispel enrage / buffs quickly.
- Do not over-open when a priority mob is alive.
- Click the lever as soon as the room reaches required energy.
