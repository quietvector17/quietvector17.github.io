# Siege of Orgrimmar Strategies

Notes and reminders for Siege of Orgrimmar (SoO). This is intentionally concise and focused on "what to do".

## Immerseus

### DPS Phase

- Burn boss.
- `Corrosive Blast`:
- One-shots any non-tank.
- Tank swap after every cast.
- Large hit: position tanks at max safe healing range away from the raid.
- Sha pools spawn under players: move out.
- `Swirl`: boss fires a moving water AoE around the room. Stay aware of the path and sidestep early.

#### Heroic: Swelling Corruption

- Boss gains `Swelling Corruption` stacks.
- Each time the boss is attacked:
- Boss loses 1 stack.
- Attacker gains a stacking debuff that increases damage taken.
- Attacker also spawns a small add.
- Guideline: avoid going above 3 to 4 stacks of the player debuff.
- Strategy:
- Hit boss until you reach ~3 stacks, then swap to AoE the small adds.
- Keep the raid fairly stacked so the tank can reliably pick up the spawned adds.

### Split Phase

- Starts when the boss reaches 0 health.
- Adds spawn and move toward the center; if they reach it, they explode and deal raid damage.
- Two add types:
- Sha Puddles (hostile):
- Stunnable and grippable.
- Players nearby gain a stacking damage buff (short duration) when they die.
- Count: 1 Sha Puddle per 4 corruption remaining when this phase begins.
- Contaminated Puddles (friendly):
- Must be healed to full.
- They move slower the more you heal them.
- When healed to full: nearby players gain a healing buff and restore some mana.
- Count: 1 Contaminated Puddle per 4 corruption cleansed from Immerseus.

### Fight Notes

- Boss does not die at 0 health.
- The corruption resource bar (100 -> 0) is the real win condition.
- Heroism on pull.
- Second potion on the second DPS phase.

## Fallen Protectors

### Overall Win Condition / Pacing

- All three bosses must die close together.
- Do not push multiple bosses to 66% or 33% simultaneously (triggers overlapping `Desperate Measures`).

#### Heroic push order (example)

- Cleave / multidot off `Rook Stonetoe` until 66%.
- Push `Sun Tenderheart` to 66% after `Rook` uses `Spinning Crane Kick`.
- Push `He Softfoot` as quickly as you can.
- Repeat once more; good time to use Heroism between 66% and 33%.
- After 33%, finish them together.

### Rook Stonetoe

- Frontal cone: face away from raid.
- Avoidable projectiles.
- Heroic: projectiles speed up with consecutive casts.
- `Spinning Crane Kick`: on a random player. Move out.

#### Desperate Measures (Rook)

- Boss disappears and spawns 3 adds.
- `Sorrow` (focus first):
- Uses `Inferno Blast`: stack with melee to split damage.
- Can be solo-soaked with immunities (similar to Static Shock on Lei Shen).
- Heroic: stack on spawn (except the He Softfoot tank).
- `Misery`: keep the tank stable (extra tank healing).
- `Gloom` (assign interrupt):
- Interrupt `Corruption Shock`.
- Heroic: all three adds share health.

### He Softfoot

- `Garrote` on random players.
- `Gouge` on tank: stuns for 6 seconds and drops aggro.
- Tank should face away to avoid it.
- When avoided: the tank is knocked back instead.
- Poisons:
- `Instant Poison`: extra tank damage.
- `Noxious Poison`: kite in a circle around the raid.

#### Desperate Measures (He)

- Spawns `Embodied Anguish` and applies `Mark of Anguish` to a random player:
- Fixates that player and deals stacking shadow damage.
- When you run out of personal CDs, transfer the mark with the Extra Action Button.
- Normal: transfer to a tank.
- Heroic: cannot throw to tanks; plan a rotation of immunities/major personals.

### Sun Tenderheart

- `Mind Sear` (random target): does not damage the target, but damages nearby players. If targeted, move away.
- Interruptible; when interrupted, she recasts on a different player.
- `Shadow Word: Bane`:
- Dispel immediately.
- If it ticks, it spreads to nearby players.
- Mass Dispel works.
- `Calamity`: 50% max HP raid hit. Chain raid CDs and personals if people are low.
- Heroic: damage increases each cast (30% -> 40% -> 50% -> ...).

#### Desperate Measures (Sun)

- Room goes dark: stand in the bubble to reduce damage taken.
- Kill two adds to end the phase (heavy cleave).
- Chain raid cooldowns here.

## Norushen

- 7 minute enrage.

### Corruption Basics

- Start at 75 corruption (Heroic: 50).
- Higher corruption = less damage to the boss (does not reduce damage to adds).
- Goal: cleanse as many DPS as possible before the 50% transition.

### Orbs / Trials ("balls")

- Typical set: send 2 DPS, 1 tank, 1 healer per set of orbs.
- DPS trial: higher corruption increases number of adds.
- Tank swap mechanic: increasing damage with consecutive casts.

#### Heroic notes

- Healers still want to get cleansed.
- Consider sending your strongest single-target DPS to maximize value after cleansing.
- In P2 (adds spawning), adds must die quickly.
- A healer should be standing where an add dies to immediately soak the pool.

### Golden Orbs (Role Details)

- Clicking an orb sends you into a personal realm.

#### Tank realm

- Survive 1 minute vs `Titanic Corruption`.
- `Titanic Smash`: avoidable frontal.
- `Hurl Corruption`: interruptible.
- Heavy burst abilities.
- After surviving: port out and set to 0 corruption.

#### Healer realm

- Several friendly NPCs + 1 `Greater Corruption`.
- `Greater Corruption`:
- Constant shadow damage to player and NPCs.
- Applies a dispellable DoT to the player.
- Kill `Greater Corruption` to exit.
- You can DPS the corruption or heal allies to increase their damage.
- After killing: port out and set to 0 corruption (healing increased when cleansed).

#### DPS realm

- Killing a mob inside spawns a copy in the outside realm with new abilities.
- Enemy count scales with corruption level.
- `Manifestation of Corruption` (kill first):
- Casts `Tear Reality` (frontal).
- Outside realm: pulses damage, melees tank, and leaves a shadow pool.
- Pool deals massive damage until picked up; picking it up gives 25 corruption (max 100).
- Move out of the raid before it dies; melee should back out to avoid instantly soaking the pool.
- Heroic: AoE pulses (including pool) increase corruption by 2.
- `Essence of Corruption` (kill after):
- Shield blocks front damage.
- Fires a line/bolt: dodge it.
- If the bolt hits the boss outside, boss gains a stacking damage buff; stun/interrupt and kill quickly.
- You must defeat every mob within 1 minute to clear corruption.
